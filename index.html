Do I need to visit your office in person?
No office visit is necessary. To keep our processing fast and efficient, we handle all document reviews, cost estimates, and consultations fully online via WhatsApp. Once we review your documents and confirm your project details, we will provide our exact office address along with a scheduled appointment for physical document drop-off or pickup. And with our new E-Apostille service, eligible documents are verified by video call and apostilled entirely online. Our 3-Step Process: 1. Send Documents: Share a clear photo or scan via WhatsApp for a quick document audit and price quote. 2. Approval & Scheduling: Once agreed, we schedule your in-person appointment or proceed remotely. 3. Legalization & Delivery: We complete all Notary Public and Governorship attestations on your behalf and deliver finished documents worldwide via DHL, FedEx, or UPS.
How long does the apostille process take in Istanbul?
Most documents are completed within 1–3 working days. With E-Apostille, your certified PDF is ready in 1–2 working days and the hard copy reaches your country in 3–7 business days. Urgent same-day handling is possible for certain procedures — confirm on WhatsApp and we will reply with the exact timing and fee in one message.
Do I need to be present in person for a Power of Attorney?
Yes. Turkish notaries require the person granting the power of attorney to sign in front of the Notary Public in person — this is a legal requirement, not a formality we can skip, and no Power of Attorney can be done online. Message us on WhatsApp first so we can confirm the document, book your appointment slot and avoid any waiting when you arrive.
