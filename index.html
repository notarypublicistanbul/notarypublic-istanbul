Questions
\n

Asked & Answered

\n
\n
\n
\n
Do I need to visit your office in Fatih?
No. We work remotely by appointment. Send a photo or scan of your document on WhatsApp; we attend the Notary Public and Governorship on your behalf, and deliver the finished documents worldwide by DHL, FedEx or UPS — no office visit needed.
\n
How long does the apostille process take in Istanbul?
Most documents are completed within 1–3 working days. Urgent same-day handling is possible for certain procedures — confirm on WhatsApp and we will reply with the exact timing and fee in one message.
\n
Do I need to be present in person for a Power of Attorney?
Yes. Turkish notaries require the person granting the power of attorney to sign in front of the Notary Public in person — this is a legal requirement, not a formality we can skip. Message us on WhatsApp first so we can confirm the document, book your appointment slot and avoid any waiting when you arrive.
