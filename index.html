Do I need to visit your office in person?
No office visit is necessary. We handle all document reviews, cost estimates, and consultations fully online via WhatsApp. Once confirmed, we share our exact office address with a scheduled appointment. And with our new E-Apostille service, eligible documents are verified by video call and apostilled entirely online.
How long does the apostille process take in Istanbul?
Most documents are completed within 1–3 working days. With E-Apostille, your certified PDF is ready in 1–2 working days and the hard copy reaches your country in 3–7 business days.
Do I need to be present in person for a Power of Attorney?
Yes. Turkish notaries require the person granting the power of attorney to sign in front of the Notary Public in person — this is a legal requirement, not a formality we can skip, and no Power of Attorney can be done online.
