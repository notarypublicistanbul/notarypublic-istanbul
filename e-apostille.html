How E-Apostille Works

Four Steps, Zero Travel

Send a High-Quality Scan

Share a clear scan of your document over WhatsApp for an instant eligibility check and quote.

Verification Video Call

Join a short live video call and record your document from every side. We confirm in real time that it is original and genuine — strictly by appointment.

Certification & Apostille

We certify your document and complete the apostille with the competent Turkish authorities on your behalf — fully online.

Worldwide Delivery

Receive your certified PDF within 1–2 working days, and the apostilled hard copy at your door — in Turkey or any country — within 3–7 business days.

Requirements — By Appointment Only

E-Apostille requires a live verification video call together with a recorded video showing every side of the document. This is how we guarantee the same legal integrity as an in-person notary appointment.

Any original, genuine document can receive an apostille — birth certificates, diplomas, court decisions, powers of attorney, commercial papers and more. Only fake or altered documents are refused.
