III — Questions

Asked & Answered

Marriage in Turkey, clear, complete, & fully transparent

Ask on WhatsApp
What documents do I need to get married in Turkey as a foreigner?
Requirements vary by nationality, but generally include a valid passport, a Certificate of No Impediment (single status certificate) from your home country, and passport photos. Message us on WhatsApp with your nationality and we'll send you the exact checklist.
What is a Certificate of No Impediment, and do I need one?
Most foreign nationals need this document — it confirms you are legally free to marry, issued by your home country's authorities. It typically needs to be translated and apostilled before Turkish authorities will accept it. We can guide you through obtaining and legalizing it.
How long does the marriage process take in Turkey?
Timing depends on your nationality, document readiness, and the municipality handling your registration. Message us on WhatsApp with your situation and we'll give you a realistic timeline.
What is the Family Book (Aile Cüzdanı)?
The Aile Cüzdanı is Turkey's official family record booklet, issued once your marriage is registered. It serves as your primary proof of marriage in Turkey and, once apostilled, is recognized internationally.
Will my Turkish marriage be recognized in my home country?
Generally yes, once the marriage record and Family Book are properly apostilled or legalized. Requirements differ by country, so it's worth confirming with your home country's authorities what they specifically require.
Can you help before my partner and I have both arrived in Turkey?
Yes. Message us on WhatsApp as early as possible — we can guide you through gathering and legalizing documents from abroad, so everything is ready by the time you arrive.
