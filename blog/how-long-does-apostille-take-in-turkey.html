How Long Does an Apostille Take in Turkey?

A practical timeline guide from TAC Notary Public Istanbul

If you've searched this question, you're probably staring down a deadline — a university enrollment, a visa appointment, a court filing abroad. Here's a straight answer, based on what we actually see processing documents for clients every week.

The short answer

A standard apostille in Turkey, once your document is ready and correctly notarized, typically takes 1 to 3 business days through the competent provincial authority. The apostille itself — a single stamped page the Hague Convention recognizes in 92 member countries — is usually the fastest part of the whole process. What actually eats up time is everything that has to happen before the apostille: translation, notarization, and sometimes additional ministry or consular steps.

A realistic breakdown

StepTypical time
Sworn translation (if required)Same day – 1 day
NotarizationSame day
Apostille certification itself1–3 business days
Additional ministry/consular legalization (non-Hague countries)5–10 business days
Courier delivery abroad3–7 business days

What actually slows it down

  • Document type. A birth certificate or diploma moves faster than a court decision or multi-page commercial contract.
  • Which authority issued it. Documents from different Turkish provinces or institutions sometimes route through different competent offices.
  • Whether it needs consular legalization instead of (or alongside) an apostille. Not every country is a Hague Convention member — for those, the process runs longer and involves extra ministry and embassy steps.
  • Travel, if you're doing this in person. Physically visiting notary offices, translation bureaus, and the apostille authority separately is usually the single biggest time cost for people outside Istanbul — or outside Turkey entirely.

Why we built a remote process

We coordinate across more than five notary offices around Istanbul, so instead of you visiting each one in sequence, we handle that routing on your behalf. For documents that qualify, our E-Apostille service verifies your original document over a live video call, then manages notarization and apostille certification fully online — with your certified PDF typically ready in 1–2 working days, and the physical apostilled copy delivered to your door in 3–7 business days, anywhere in the world.

The honest caveat

If your destination country isn't a Hague Convention member, an apostille alone won't be accepted — you'll need consular legalization instead, which takes longer and involves the relevant embassy. If you're not sure which applies to you, the quickest way to find out is to just send us the document and destination country — we'll tell you exactly what it needs and how long it will realistically take.
